What to share about your setting

Bonaire's diver-focused accommodation and exposed coastal spaces call for appropriate setup, privacy and sun protection. The most useful information is a named accommodation, exact entrance, permission for a visiting therapist and a suitable private treatment room.

Choosing a treatment

Guests may enquire about Swedish relaxation massage, deep tissue massage or couples massage. A two-person session requires two professionals and suitable space; none is guaranteed by submitting a form.

Before you commit

Confirm therapist qualifications, local availability, realistic travel and the full price. These are enquiries, not published coverage guarantees. If your hotel has an in-house spa or restricts outside providers, its rules take priority.

BONAIRE · SOROBON

Private massage planning in Sorobon

Sorobon sits beside Lac Bay on Bonaire's windward side and is strongly associated with watersports stays. This area is different from the sheltered west-coast hotel corridor.

Venue suitability

Wind, sand and exposed beaches can make outdoor treatments inappropriate. A guest should identify a private, sheltered room and confirm that the watersports lodge or rental allows an outside practitioner.

Treatment options

Relaxation after a watersports day may be requested at an approved private stay, while deep-tissue, couples or group appointments need the right qualified staff and equipment. Public waterfront areas should not be presumed available.

Travel and provider access

The eastern route to Sorobon differs from north-coast and town journeys. Request the actual lodge or rental address, not only Lac Bay, before a local appointment time is proposed.

Sorobon massage question

Can a session be held on an open Sorobon beach? Only where the property permits it and the provider confirms a safe, sheltered and private treatment environment.
